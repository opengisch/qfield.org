## Workflows
- Wildlife observations and telemetry  
- Habitat mapping and suitability assessments  
- Protected areas surveys and monitoring
- Transect and track recording for movement and survey effort
- Audio capture for birds, amphibians, and other acoustic species
- Repeat survey points for monitoring populations over time

## Integrated with QGIS and QFieldCloud
Prepare symbology and forms in [QGIS](https://qgis.org), then sync projects with [QFieldCloud](https://qfield.cloud) to collaborate across teams. Crews work offline for as long as the survey takes, and conflict-safe merges keep a whole team working the same area without losing records.
