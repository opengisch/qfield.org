## Field workflows

Planning departments use QField across the planning cycle:

- Site surveys and pre-application assessments
- Zoning compliance checks against the overlay that governs the parcel
- Building permit inspections and development monitoring
- Enforcement documentation with dated, geolocated photo evidence
- Land use surveys and plan monitoring over time
- Public consultation notes captured against specific locations

## The plan, on the parcel, in your hand

Planning decisions are made in the office and tested on the ground, and the gap between the two is where enforcement cases are lost. QField closes it by putting the zoning overlay, the cadastral parcel, the permit record, and the aerial imagery on the tablet, available offline. An inspector standing on a site can see what is permitted there before deciding whether what is built matches it.

The evidence follows the same logic. A photograph taken in QField carries its coordinate, its timestamp, and the inspector who took it, attached to the feature it documents. Assembling an enforcement pack stops being an archaeology exercise across a camera roll, an email thread, and a notebook.

## One workflow with QGIS and QFieldCloud

Prepare zoning layers, parcels, and inspection forms in [QGIS](https://qgis.org). Push the project to every device through [QFieldCloud](https://qfield.cloud). Planners and inspectors work offline and sync back into the same project the office plans from, with conflict-safe merges.
