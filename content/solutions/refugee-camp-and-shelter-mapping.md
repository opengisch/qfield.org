## Field workflows

Site planners and camp management teams use QField to keep an accurate, shared picture of a site:

- Shelter inventories with IDs, condition, and occupancy details
- Site planning, plot layout, and allocation
- Water, sanitation, and hygiene facility mapping
- Health posts, schools, distribution points, and other services
- Drainage, access routes, and hazard mapping
- Protection and safety mapping, including lighting and risk areas

## Keeping pace with a changing site

Camps and displacement sites rarely stand still. New arrivals, relocations, flooding, and construction change the layout week by week, and a map that was right last month can send a distribution team to the wrong place today.

QField lets the people who are on site every day keep the map current. Shelters and services are added and updated on the device, with photos and structured attributes, and the changes flow back into one shared project. Because the form is defined once in QGIS, a record captured by one partner looks the same as a record captured by another, so the data can be combined without a round of cleaning.

## One workflow with QGIS and QFieldCloud

Prepare the site project in [QGIS](https://qgis.org), including drone imagery, plot layouts, and service layers. Share it with every partner through [QFieldCloud](https://qfield.cloud), with permissions that match who needs to see what. Teams work offline on site and sync when they can.
