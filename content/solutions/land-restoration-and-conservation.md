## Workflows
- Restoration site mapping and monitoring  
- Habitat assessments and invasive species tracking  
- Community programs and stewardship records
- Planting block and treatment area capture as polygons
- Before-and-after monitoring at fixed locations
- Photo documentation against sites and individual features

## Built for monitoring recovery over time

Restoration is measured in years, not a single visit. QField stores each observation as a structured record with its location, attributes, photos, and capture time. On the next visit, the previous records load straight onto the device, and the live GPS position guides crews back to the same sites. The result is a consistent record of recovery rather than a pile of one-off surveys.

## Integrated with QGIS and QFieldCloud
Design forms and symbology in [QGIS](https://qgis.org), then sync and review via [QFieldCloud](https://qfield.cloud). Crews work offline for as long as the survey takes, and conflict-safe merges keep multiple crews working the same site without overwriting each other.
