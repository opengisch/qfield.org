How KAN Territory & IT supported survey workflows in Costa Rica

**Partner:** KAN Territory & IT
**Technology:** QField Cloud
**Client:** National Institute of Statistics and Census of Costa Rica (INEC Costa Rica)

### Context

National-scale field operations require much more than mobile data collection. They demand **planning, coordination, reliability, and real-time visibility** across complex territories and diverse teams.

The National Institute of Statistics and Census of Costa Rica (INEC) is responsible for conducting nationwide surveys that involve multiple field teams operating simultaneously, often in areas with limited connectivity and under strict data quality requirements.

INEC needed a solution that could support these challenges while remaining **scalable, reliable, and easy to operate** for both coordinators and field staff.

### The Challenge

INEC faced several operational challenges:

- Planning and coordinating fieldwork across the entire country
- Managing multiple teams, users, and permission levels
- Ensuring reliable offline data collection and secure synchronization
- Monitoring survey progress in near real time
- Maintaining full data traceability and consistency

Traditional workflows based on spreadsheets and fragmented tools limited operational control and made it difficult to detect issues early or adapt plans during field execution.

### The Solution

As an official partner, **KAN Territory & IT** implemented a **Field Planning and Execution System** built on **QField Cloud**, positioning it as the **central collaboration and synchronization layer** between office-based planning and field execution.

QField Cloud enabled:

- Centralized project and data management
- Organization-, team-, and role-based access control
- Reliable offline-first field data collection
- Secure merging and synchronization of data from multiple teams

On top of this foundation, KAN designed planning workflows that allowed INEC to:

- Assign field tasks by geographic area and operational unit
- Structure field campaigns and priorities
- Monitor operational progress in near real time
- Maintain traceability of field activities
- Integrate field results into analytical and visualization environments

Rather than replacing existing systems, QField Cloud became the **connective layer** that linked survey design, field execution, and decision-making processes.

### Results & Impact

By adopting QField Cloud as part of its operational ecosystem, INEC achieved:

- Improved coordination of nationwide field teams
- Faster identification of operational bottlenecks
- Reduced manual intervention and error-prone processes
- Stronger data consistency and quality
- A scalable model ready for future survey campaigns

Field teams benefited from a **clearer and more intuitive workflow**, while coordinators gained the visibility needed to steer operations effectively.

### Partnership Value

This collaboration demonstrates how **QField Cloud**, combined with strong planning and integration expertise, can evolve from a data collection tool into a **comprehensive field operations platform**.

For KAN Territory & IT, the project reinforces its role as a partner that bridges **technology and real-world operations**, helping institutions unlock the full potential of spatial data in mission-critical fieldwork scenarios.

### Key Takeaway

QField Cloud provides a solid foundation for scalable field operations, and when combined with the right planning strategy, enables **reliable, accessible, and well-orchestrated spatial workflows at national scale**.
