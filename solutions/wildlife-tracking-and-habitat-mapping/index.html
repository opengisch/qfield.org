Common challenges

  • Remote field sites
  • Need for standard taxonomy and protocols
  • Media management and traceability
  • Observations written in notebooks with no precise location
  • GPS drift placing sightings in the wrong location under tree cover
  • Sensitive species locations that need to be captured carefully and kept controlled

Why teams choose QField

  • Used by conservation and research teams
  • Offline and high-accuracy GNSS support
  • Captures observations, tracks, photos, and audio in one structured record
  • Constrained forms keep species names and protocols consistent across surveyors
  • Open source, runs on Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, and Linux

Workflows

  • Wildlife observations and telemetry
  • Habitat mapping and suitability assessments
  • Protected areas surveys and monitoring
  • Transect and track recording for movement and survey effort
  • Audio capture for birds, amphibians, and other acoustic species
  • Repeat survey points for monitoring populations over time

Integrated with QGIS and QFieldCloud

Prepare symbology and forms in QGIS , then sync projects with QFieldCloud to collaborate across teams. Crews work offline for as long as the survey takes, and conflict-safe merges keep a whole team working the same area without losing records.

Frequently asked questions

Can we work offline for long periods?

Yes. Store basemaps and data locally and sync later via QFieldCloud.

Do you support sensitive data handling?

Manage permissions in QFieldCloud and control data sharing.

Can I record observations as points and movement as tracks?

Yes. QField handles point, line, and polygon layers in one project. Capture sightings as points and record movement or transects as tracks against the same project.

Does QField capture audio for acoustic species?

Yes. QField records audio as an attachment field, alongside photos and video, stored against the observation with its location and capture time.

How accurate is positioning under tree cover?

Built-in tablet GPS typically gives accuracy of a few metres. For survey-grade positioning, pair QField with a Bluetooth GNSS receiver. RTK corrections bring positioning to centimetres.

Can I enforce a standard species list and survey protocol?

Yes. Configure value-relation widgets for species lists and constraints for counts and protocol fields in QGIS. QField enforces them on the tablet, so surveyors cannot enter invalid values.

Can multiple surveyors work the same study area at once?

Yes. With QFieldCloud each surveyor gets their own offline copy. Changes merge automatically on sync.

About QField

QField is the leading professional fieldwork app used in enterprise settings for efficient geospatial data collection and management. As a Digital Public Good, QField not only excels in professional applications but also contributes significantly to advancing at least six of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) , promoting a more sustainable and equitable future.

Open-source

QField is released under the GNU Public License (GPL) version 2 or later. Developing QField under this license means that you can inspect and modify the source code and guarantees that you will always have access to a QGIS based field data collection app that is free of cost and can be freely modified.
